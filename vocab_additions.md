# Vocab additions (unverified)

Additions to `sinhala_study_pack.md`. Everything here is unverified, so check it with a teacher or a native speaker before relying on it.

Romanisation follows the pack's conventions: "th" as in thaaththa, doubled vowels for long sounds, and (a) for the "a" in apple.

**Source key:**

- **web:** matches at least one phrasebook or reference site.
- **ai:** drafted from general knowledge or built from the pack's grammar patterns. Lower confidence.

## Verb negatives (present tense)

Rule: replace the -nawaa ending with -nne naa. For example, kanawaa becomes kanne naa.
