## 6. Grammar rules from the lessons

- **Word order:** Subject + Object + Verb. Example: *Mama bath kanawaa.* (I eat rice.)
- **Possession `-ge`:** mama → mage (my), oyaa → oyaage (your).
- **"Also / too" `-th`:** mama → mamath, oyaa → oyaath.
- **Question marker `-da`:** added to the end (mokakda? kohomada?).
- **"To" `-ta`:** kadayata (to the shop), iskolayata (to school), Lankaawata (to Sri Lanka), oyaata (to you).
- **"In" or "of" a place:** take the place name, remove the final "a" sound, add "e". Sri Lankaawa → Sri Lankaawe (in / of Sri Lanka). Lankaawe kenek = a Sri Lankan. Sri Lankaawe minissu = people of Sri Lanka.
- **Place before the verb:** mention the place first, then the verb, with the "e" ending on the place. Example: *Mama d(a)n Chiinaye igena gannawaa.*
- **koheda vs kohenda:** *Oyaa koheda?* = Where are you? *Oyaa kohenda?* = Where are you from? Answer *kohenda* with your country.
- **eth** = "but". Example idea from the slides: He is learning English, but I'm learning Sinhala.
